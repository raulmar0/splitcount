# Buenpar logo

Generated using the built-in ImageGen tool, logo-brand mode. The mark is the product asset; the wordmark is live Manrope text for clear rendering in both themes.

## Initial generation prompt

Create an original, production-ready app brand symbol for Buenpar, a Spanish shared-expense app for friends. The idea is two equal contributions becoming one clear account. Design one compact, memorable lowercase-b-inspired monogram made of two balanced interlocking rounded bands with two distinct equal negative-space counters. Friendly but precise; more like a carefully cut contemporary identity mark than a bank, crypto token or generic infinity symbol. Flat solid sage-mint #8EB99A only, no gradients, shading, texture, glow, outlines, shadows or mockup. The mark must read clearly at 24px and have a strong silhouette. Center it tightly within a square canvas with roughly 12 percent safe margin. Transparent background with actual alpha. One symbol only, no lettering, no wordmark, no captions, no currency signs, no arrows, no decorative container, no watermark. Crisp vector-like edges and optically balanced geometric curves.
